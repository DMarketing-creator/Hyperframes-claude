# SMILE — "The Stack" · 15-second live-action shoot plan

One performer, one desk, four setups. Phone-friendly. About 60 minutes on set.
No dialogue. All text (titles, voice-over) is added in the edit.

---

## 1. What you need

**People**
- 1 performer: woman in her 30s, natural look. Wears **glasses** (needed for the reflection in Shot 1; clear-lens glasses are fine).
- 1 person to operate the camera and hand-place paper (can be you).

**Camera**
- A recent phone, shooting **4K, 24 fps**, locked exposure and focus (AE/AF lock).
- Lens: use the **2x lens (about 50 mm equivalent)** for Shots 1 and 4, the **1x lens (about 24–28 mm)** for Shot 3. Do **not** use Portrait mode (it fakes depth of field and warps hair and hands).
- Shutter: if your app allows, **1/48 s** (180°). If not, avoid very bright light so the phone keeps natural motion blur.
- Tripod, plus a clamp or boom arm for the overhead shot (Shot 2). Books under a tripod head also work.
- Do **not** use digital zoom, beauty filters or HDR video.

**Props**
- A laptop. **Cover its logo with matte black tape.** No visible brand marks anywhere (laptop, mug, lanyard, notebooks).
- About 150 printed sheets that look like resumes but contain **no readable text and no real names** (use greyed bars, "lorem" filler or blurred layouts). Never print real applicant data.
- One plain ceramic mug, one desk lamp, a few pens, a sticky-note pad, a small plant.
- White foam board (bounce), black card (flag), a small LED panel (dusk shot).

**Screens** (I can supply both as looping video you play full-screen on the laptop)
- **Screen A:** an endless scroll of plain white rows. No readable text.
- **Screen B:** a clean light-mode dashboard: three candidate rows, circular percentage score badges, soft blue primary colour. Legible shapes, no readable names, no product name.

---

## 2. Framing rules (apply to EVERY shot)

- **Shoot vertical, 9:16.** Turn on a grid overlay.
- **Top-left corner stays clear:** about **22% of the width × 12% of the height**. Plain, low-detail, out of focus. No faces, no text, no high-contrast edges. If in doubt, flag it with black card or move a plant out of there.
- **Subject weighted centre-right.**
- **Lower third stays open** for text. Keep the bottom third low-detail (desk surface, floor, soft blur).
- **Handheld** only where noted, with small natural drift. Everything else locked off.

---

## 3. Shot by shot

### Shot 1 · 0:00–0:03 · Extreme close-up, scrolling
| | |
|---|---|
| Setup | 2x lens, very close, tripod or braced hand. Fills frame with her thumb on the trackpad, glasses just in frame. |
| Action | Thumb scrolls **continuously**, steady speed, never pauses. Laptop plays Screen A. |
| The detail | Moving white rows reflected in her glasses. Angle the laptop so the screen reflects. If it doesn't read, hold a bright tablet just out of frame, low. |
| Light | Window as soft key from the side, laptop glow on her hand. No lens flare. |
| Take | 3 takes, 8–10 s each. Pick the one where the reflection reads. |
| Corner | Top-left = defocused wall or dark desk edge. |

### Shot 2 · 0:03–0:07 · Top-down, the stack grows
| | |
|---|---|
| Setup A (preferred) | High angle about 70° from behind her shoulder so her **hands and the desk** are in frame, her forehead entering at the top-right. Lock off. |
| Setup B (if A is awkward) | True top-down for the hands, then a 2-second **side-profile insert** of her hand pressing her forehead. |
| Action | A hand places one sheet on the stack. Then another. Then faster, with no pause. The other hand rises to her forehead near the end. |
| Speed-up | **Do not speed up in camera.** Shoot at natural pace over about 12 s with 12+ sheets and build the rhythm yourself. In edit I time-remap about 3x, no frame blending, so it stays photoreal. |
| Stack | Start with 4–5 sheets already there and keep the stack placement identical every take. |
| Light | Same window key. Add a white foam bounce on the far side. Soft shadows only. |
| Corner | Top-left of frame = bare desk or blurred keyboard. |

### Shot 3 · 0:07–0:11 · Static wide, dusk, "the stillness is the point"
| | |
|---|---|
| Setup | 1x lens, **locked off**, tripod, wide. She is small in frame, centre-right. Desk mid-frame, **dark floor or desk in the lower third**. |
| Action | She sits at the desk, lit by her screen. **Nothing happens.** She breathes, maybe blinks once. Hold. Roll **at least 15 s**. |
| Light | Shoot at blue hour, or fake it: close the blinds, lights off, a cool-blue window, **laptop on about 60% brightness plus a small LED panel low behind it** to lift her face. Turn off all overhead practicals. Keep ISO at or below 1600. |
| Corner | Top-left = dark ceiling or blind. Check it is plain. |
| Mood | Empty chairs and dark desks around her. No people in the background. |

### Shot 4 · 0:11–0:15 · Hard cut to morning
| | |
|---|---|
| Setup | **Same desk, same camera position as Shot 3, but move in a little** so the screen and her hand are readable. Keep the same height. |
| Desk | **No paper at all.** Only the laptop, the mug (lifted into frame), a pen and the plant. |
| Screen | Laptop plays **Screen B** (dashboard, three rows, circular score badges). Soft, not readable. |
| Action | Her hand lifts the coffee cup into frame, relaxed. **She is not looking at the screen.** She glances toward the window or off-frame. |
| Light | Warm morning window as key, soft, higher and brighter than Shot 3. Subtle steam off the cup. |
| Take | 3 takes, 8 s each. |

## 4. Continuity checklist
- Same desk, same laptop angle, same plant position in Shots 3 and 4.
- Same hair, same glasses. A **different top** in Shot 4 is good (it reads as the next day).
- Camera height and lens for Shots 3 and 4 must match so the hard cut feels like the same room.
- Keep the stack of paper **off screen entirely** in Shot 4.
- No logos, no readable text on paper, mug or screen.

---

## 5. Look
- Neutral grade, faint cool-blue shadows, warm skin tones.
- Mild film grain, unretouched skin, no smoothing, no HDR look.
- No lens flare, no whip pans, no drone shots.

---

## 6. Sound to record on set (no dialogue)
Record **60 seconds of room tone** at each look (day and dusk), plus:
- Trackpad clicks and thumb scroll (close mic, or phone near the laptop)
- Paper slides, single sheets and a bigger stack drop
- Air-conditioning
- Distant keyboard
- Chair creak, mug set down

**Sound design in the edit (I'll build this):**
- Shots 1–3: layers build in density: trackpad → accelerating paper → air-con swell with distant keyboard.
- **Hard cut to near-silence on the Shot 4 picture cut**, then **one warm tone** fading in. No music bed before that.
